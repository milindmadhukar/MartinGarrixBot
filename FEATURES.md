# STMPD RCRDS Bot — Features

A community-built Discord bot for STMPD RCRDS — turning listeners into an active community.

## Music & Catalogue

- **Full label catalogue** — every release across the STMPD RCRDS roster
- **Lyrics lookup** — search any song by name, with search-as-you-type suggestions
- **Streaming links** — Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube and Beatport on a single card
- **Track detail** — BPM, musical key, genre, mix name, release date and length
- **Sing-along** — the bot drops a lyric, members race to complete the next line for points
- **Lyric quiz** — guess the song from its lyrics, across four difficulty tiers
- **Song anniversaries** — "X years ago today" release throwbacks

## Release & News Notifications

- **New releases** — label drops announced the moment they land
- **YouTube** — new uploads from the STMPD channel and roster artists
- **Beatport** — new releases across the label and its artists
- **Reddit** — new posts from the community subreddit
- **Tour & shows** — new dates announced, with ticket links
- **Per-feed routing** — each feed gets its own channel and an opt-in ping role
- **Hands-off announcements** — detected and posted automatically, around the clock,
  with nothing for the team to publish manually

## 24/7 Radio

- **Always-on radio** — the STMPD catalogue streaming in a voice channel
- **Now playing** — artwork, track info and streaming links on demand
- **Live channel status** — the voice channel name shows the current track
- **Vote to skip** — the listeners in the channel decide
- **Fully automatic** — set the channel once and it runs itself, reconnecting after any outage

## Activeness & Levels

- **Earn by taking part** — XP for messaging, sing-along wins and quiz answers
- **Level-ups** — generated rank cards showing level, rank and progress
- **Automatic roles** — members unlock roles as they level up
- **Leaderboards** — levels, activity and points

## Points & Rewards (STMPD Coins)

- **One currency** — earned through activity, spent on rewards
- **Merch perks** — points redeem for a discount at the STMPD store (e.g. 5% off)
- **Exclusive channels** — active members unlock members-only channels
- **Early access** — announcements, drops and presales ahead of everyone else
- **Member to member** — gift points to other members
- **No real money** — points are earned, never bought

## Moderation & Safety

- **Full action set** — kick, ban, tempban, softban and unban
- **Timeouts** — timeout with a duration, and untimeout
- **Moderation history** — every action reasoned and recorded per member
- **Mod-log channel** — all moderator actions mirrored for transparency
- **Join & leave logging** — with account age and live member count
- **Message logging** — edits with before/after, and deletions with full content
- **Configurable moderator role** — fits whatever staff structure the server already has

## Fun & Utility

- **Member lookup** — avatars and profile info
- **8-ball** — for the hard decisions
- **Status check** — bot latency and health
